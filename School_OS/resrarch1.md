
## CAMEROON MARKET BUSINESS IDEAS (Research)

### Smaller than School OS (Solo-buildable, 50K-500K CFA startup)

1. **NjangiOS — Digital Njangi/Tontine Operating System**

   - Full operating system: automatic contribution reminders, transparent payout lottery, penalty enforcement, member history, dispute resolution, WhatsApp notifications. 9 in 10 Cameroonians borrow to survive. Njangi is literally how families stay afloat. Charge 2,000-5,000 CFA/group/month.
2. **NkwaGuard — Hospital Payment Plan Installer**

   - 68% of health spending is out-of-pocket. <2% of Cameroonians have insurance. Hospital detention is documented. Build a tool hospitals install at intake: calculates the bill, offers installment plans via Mobile Money, auto-enrolls patients in CBHI.
3. **Quittancier — Rent Receipt & Lease Generator**

   - Every landlord needs receipts. Tenants need proof of payment for visa applications, school enrollment, employment. WhatsApp bot: tenant sends payment confirmation, gets a PDF receipt. 500 CFA/receipt or 5,000 CFA/month unlimited.
4. **MarchéPrice — Market Price Intelligence via SMS/USSD**

   - Farmers lose 30-50% to middlemen because they don't know actual prices. Price reporting network: market women text daily prices, you aggregate and sell access via SMS for 100 CFA/query or 2,000 CFA/month. No internet needed.

### Similar Scale to School OS (Team-buildable, 500K-5M CFA MVP)

5. **BenskinOS — Moto Taxi Fleet Management**

   - 100K+ moto taxis in Cameroon. GPS trip tracking, fare optimization, micro-insurance, micro-loans for bike maintenance. Revenue: per-driver subscription + insurance commissions.
6. **ClinikOS — Private Clinic Operating System**

   - Private clinics are where Cameroonians actually go. They run on paper. Build: patient management, drug inventory, billing, lab results, insurance claims, WhatsApp appointment booking. 15,000-30,000 CFA/month. 3,000+ private clinics in Cameroon.
7. **ContratCA — OHADA Contract & Document Factory**

   - Lawyers cost 50,000-200,000 CFA per contract. Bilingual (EN/FR) contract generator: rental agreements, employment contracts, supplier agreements. WhatsApp bot. 2,000-5,000 CFA per document.
8. **EgliseOS — Church/Mosque Management Platform**

   - 10,000+ churches in Cameroon. Track tithes, manage events, member directory, financial reporting. 10,000-20,000 CFA/month. Zero competition, high willingness to pay.

### Bigger than School OS (Requires team + capital, 5M+ CFA)

9. **DechetsChain — Formalized Waste-to-Cash Platform**

   - Formalize the informal recycling chain. Household waste pickup scheduling, pricing engine, recycling company marketplace. Transaction fees + recycling credits + corporate ESG partnerships.
10. **PropTechCM — Digital Property Cadaster**

    - IMF recommended this for Cameroon in 2026. Property registration with satellite/GIS mapping, valuation engine, tax calculation. Government-contract play.
11. **AgriLedger — Farmer Cooperative Financial Infrastructure**

    - Cooperative accounting, member contribution tracking, crop-sharing calculators, micro-loan management. 4,000+ agricultural cooperatives in Cameroon.
12. **SchoolFeed — School Feeding Supply Chain**

    - Supplier marketplace for school food, delivery tracking, quality inspection, payment escrow. Procurement platform. Revenue: transaction fees.

### Safest Bets (Lowest Risk)

- **NjangiOS** — Cultural behavior already exists, digitizing it. Zero customer education.
- **Quittancier** — Landlords pay immediately. No behavior change.
- **ContratCA** — Every business needs contracts. Lawyers are expensive. Simple value prop.

### Highest Ceiling

- **BenskinOS** — 100K+ users, recurring revenue, insurance commission upside.
- **ClinikOS** — Healthcare is where the money is, clinics are desperate.
- **PropTechCM** — Government contracts, IMF backing, structural need.

## CAMEROON MARKET DATA POINTS

- 9 in 10 Cameroonians borrow to survive rising costs (Kasi Insight Q1 2025)
- 68.26% of health spending is out-of-pocket (global average: 16.36%)
- <2% of Cameroonians have formal health insurance
- 443,524 SMEs in Cameroon, most in tertiary/informal sector
- 100K+ moto taxis (benskins) earning 100 CFA/trip minimum
- 60B FCFA lost annually to energy theft
- Only 38 government services online (target 50 by 2026)
- Housing deficit: 2.5 million units
- Phone theft + mobile money scams = urban epidemic
- Solar adoption exploding (bamenda: 1.5M CFA household setup)
- Power cuts cost businesses 3-5% annual revenue
- $145M annual VC inflow to Cameroon (+60% growth)
- 850+ active tech startups
- Startup Act: labeled startups pay 0% corporate income tax for 5 years
- Agro-processing tax credits: 25-80% of invested amounts
